# Mastering the V60 Pour Over Technique

> [!note]
> This sample article has NO frontmatter and can be safely deleted.

Manual pour over brewing allows total control over every aspect of extraction. By controlling water flow and agitation, you can highlight origin-specific notes in specialty coffee.

Compare this technique to high-pressure brewing in [[espresso-extraction|The Physics of Espresso Extraction]].

Tags: #coffee #brewing #filter-methods

## The Recipe

- **Coffee**: 15g washed Ethiopian Yirgacheffe
- **Water**: 250g filtered brew water at $93^\circ\text{C}$
- **Ratio**: 1:16.6

Pour in gentle concentric circles, avoiding the paper walls directly, until total brew time reaches ==2 minutes and 45 seconds==.

- [x] Rinse paper filter with hot water
- [x] Bloom with 45g water for 45 seconds
- [ ] Complete continuous pour to 250g
